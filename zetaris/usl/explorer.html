Where the data comes from

Two SEC APIs feed four Zetaris REST tables. Each USL table is one activation query over them. Dashed lines are the foreign keys.

Facts whose filing is outside SEC's recent list

The fact.accn rule fails when a fact cites a filing missing from filings.recent, which holds only recent filings. Older facts cannot be traced to their filing from this source alone.
